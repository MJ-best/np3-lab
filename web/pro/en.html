Questions

How do I pay?

Sign in to the web app and press Get Pro to go to the Lemon Squeezy checkout. Pay by card, Apple Pay and more; the receipt comes by email.

Can I cancel or get a refund?

Cancel anytime under Settings › Account › Manage subscription; Pro stays on until the end of the paid period. Ask within 7 days of paying for a full refund.

What happens to my links after Pro ends?

Links you've made keep working. New links can be made while Pro is active.

Can I share community recipes?

No. Community recipes such as those by Nikon creators belong to their creators, so you can share only recipes you made or imported yourself.

Will free features shrink?

No. Everything so far stays free, with no ads. Pro is for new features.
